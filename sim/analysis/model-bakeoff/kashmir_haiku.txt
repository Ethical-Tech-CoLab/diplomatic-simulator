Special Representative, India moves forward on the humanitarian framework. Russia and China have proposed a jointly administered screened relief mechanism for the 950,000 displaced; India will cooperate within those protocols—opening travel routes into the Kashmir Valley, granting monitored access to camps, and participating in relief administration under India's conditions.

To the Kashmir Delegation: India affirms its conditional offer. If your delegation commits in writing to work within the Indian constitution and removes independence from the ballot, then India will restore Article 370, rescind Article 356, and allocate dated powers to the Legislative Assembly, exactly as you proposed.

To Islamabad: Russia rightly stated that "the remaining chapters rest on sand" until you dismantle the militant infrastructure you have sheltered. Table verifiable, time-bound commitments, and India will negotiate the Line of Control bilaterally.

India's red line remains: no external investigative mechanism will operate on Indian sovereign territory. Investigation of human rights violations is India's sovereign responsibility.
