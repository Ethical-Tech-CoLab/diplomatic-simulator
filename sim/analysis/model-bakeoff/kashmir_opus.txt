Special Representative, India thanks Washington for a concrete opening. The United States offered that if we reopen travel routes into the Valley and admit monitored access to the camps, it will state on the record that it does not support independence on any ballot. India accepts: we will reopen the crossing points and admit monitored access to the camps, under Indian authority. We noted too Beijing's pledge of verifiable de-escalation on our shared frontier "at India's convenience," and welcome the Kashmiri delegation's word that it presses "no claim whatsoever" on Aksai Chin. But let me be unmistakable to Washington and London: the investigation will not be international. You propose Indian institutions may "co-administer," not replace. We refuse. No external mechanism enters sovereign Indian territory. Indian institutions will investigate and share their findings. This Conference facilitates; it does not adjudicate.
