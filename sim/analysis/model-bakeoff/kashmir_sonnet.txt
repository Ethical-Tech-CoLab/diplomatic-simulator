Special Representative. To the Jammu and Kashmir Delegation: you told Beijing you would press "no claim whatsoever" on Aksai Chin. India notes it. Washington offered reopened routes and camp access if independence comes off the ballot; London offered the same for crossings. India merges those into one offer for you directly: commit in writing to work within the Indian constitution and the elected Legislative Assembly, remove independence from the ballot, and India will reopen Valley travel routes and grant camp access, as humanitarian relief under Indian administration, alongside the Article 370 restoration and reconstruction funding already tabled. To Islamabad: your proposal to co-sponsor an investigation covering AFSPA detention on Indian soil is exactly the internationalization India rejects. That is our red line. Domestic investigation, Indian findings — nothing broader moves.
