Mr. Chairman, the Democratic People's Republic of Korea acknowledges significant movement. The Russian Federation has stated it will verify the freeze through IAEA inspectors—precisely the reciprocity we require. We accept the People's Republic of China's proposal for monitored suspension at Yongbyon and our declared test sites. Here is our concrete package. If humanitarian assistance is delivered immediately, financial-institution sanctions are lifted as Russia and China have urged, and Japan-Republic of Korea-United States exercises are suspended, then the DPRK will freeze all nuclear and missile development, readmit the IAEA as a step within an agreed sequence, and with Seoul enter peace-treaty negotiations. But we must state plainly: humanitarian assistance and sanctions relief are not rewards for dismantlement. Our people's crisis demands immediate response independent of sequencing. Respect and recognition precede disarmament. We came to Beijing expecting results. We remain prepared to deliver them.
