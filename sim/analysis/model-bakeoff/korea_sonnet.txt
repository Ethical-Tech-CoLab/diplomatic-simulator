Mr. Chairman, we heard Washington, Seoul, Tokyo and Moscow each declare, in Washington's words, that this table wants the deterrent "gone" on a timetable we never set. Let the record stand: no reduction of our nuclear and missile programs proceeds without concessions and guarantees from those who threaten us — that line does not move. Yet we came prepared to act. If WHO-channeled humanitarian assistance is delivered now and financial-institution sanctions are lifted immediately, as Beijing and Moscow both urge, the DPRK freezes all nuclear and missile development and testing against suspension of every Japan-ROK-U.S. exercise, and readmits IAEA monitors as a step within that process — verified, as China proposed at Yongbyon. To Tokyo: the abduction question is closed here; raise Japan's reparations owed to our people first, or raise it bilaterally, not at this table.
